Story-Driven Horror Survival

Infernal Ritual

"Infernal Ritual is a story-driven horror survival game where the player becomes trapped inside a relative’s house after uncovering traces of an ancient and forbidden immortal ritual. As the mystery unfolds, the player must survive escalating paranormal events while searching for a way to escape the cursed location.

Gameplay Mechanics:

  • ▹ Exploration-driven survival horror gameplay.

  • ▹ Puzzle solving based on environmental clues, notes, and hidden messages.
  • ▹ Dynamic paranormal events that increase tension and unpredictability.
  • ▹ Demon encounters involving chase, stealth, and survival mechanics.
  • ▹ Hiding mechanics, including lockers and safe spots, to evade demonic entities.

Multiplayer Cooperative Horror & Networking

Backroom: The Dark Sacrifice

"Backroom: The Dark Sacrifice" is a multiplayer cooperative horror game where players must work together to escape the mysterious Backrooms. The environment is filled with puzzles, hostile entities, and constant tension. Players must explore, solve puzzles collaboratively, and survive while being hunted by ghosts that roam the maze-like corridors.

Gameplay Mechanics:

  • 🌐 Multiplayer Replication System: Synchronizes gameplay, character positions, and world events across connected players.
  • 🖥️ Local Server Hosting: Enables seamless peer-to-peer session creation and joining.
  • 🎲 Randomized Puzzle Generation: Increases replayability with unique layout variations each session.
  • 🧩 Collaborative Puzzle Solving: Requires co-op coordination to unlock paths and progress.
  • 👻 AI Ghost Behavior System: Features intelligent entities programmed with sensory detection and hunting loops.
  • ⚖️ Dynamic Decision-Making: Strategic choices that directly impact survival and rescue operations.
  • 🚪 Atmospheric Exploration: Maze navigation filled with psychological horror and high tension.
